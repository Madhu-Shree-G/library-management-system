LIBRARY POLICY

Simple rules.
Clear deadlines.

01

14-day loan

Every book gets a due date exactly 14 days after the moment you borrow it. The profile shows the exact date and time.

02

Maximum 3 books

Each member can have up to three active loans at one time.

03

₹5 late fine per day

If a book passes its due time, a ₹5 fine is calculated for each started late day until it is returned.

04

Return from your profile

Your profile shows the live fine, overdue days and return button for every active loan.

05

One book, one active loan

A book cannot be borrowed by another member while it is already on loan.

06

Member account required

You can browse the catalogue without an account, but signing in is required to borrow or return books.
